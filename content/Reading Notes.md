### Equilibrium theory of biodiversity

#theory
[[storchCradlesMuseumsDisequilibria2026]] argue biodiversity in fundamentally at equilibrium over latitudinal and evolutionary scales. [[pieWhereEvidenceBiodiversity2026]] argues there is no evidence for equilibrium or for the associated feedback between diversity and diversification. No mention of the stability of the equilibrium, even if it exists. THe idea of disequilibrium or historical effects can be linked to the instabiilty of equilibria or to non-autonomous systems where no equilibrium exists.
