> in a recent global meta-analysis of plant litter decomposition studies \[4] only 10.2% of studies enabled macrofaunal access to litter. The meta-analysis results largely concord with the canonical decomposition paradigm, showing that substrate quality and climate are the major drivers of decomposition \[4]. Yet, when we split the case studies by whether litterbags allowed access to macrofauna, we found fundamentally different results. When macrofauna are excluded, significant differences in litter mass loss rates are observed between wet and dry climates; these differences are minimal when macrofauna are included.
