#### Sampling design (details)

##### Transects

- 15m
- Habitat treatment: spurs or back-spurs
- Reef (random): North or South
- Fish survey: 2min acclimation; 5min one-way travel along transect. Count parrot fish by species (stoplight, queen, princess) and phase (terminal, initial) within a 4m corridor. average between observers.
- Quadrats: 30x30cm every 3m. %cover of live coral and macroalgae.

##### Grazing rate

- Same treatments as for [[#Transects]]
- identify species and phase; follow for 3min or until lost. Record number of bites. Average between observers.

#### Other ideas
