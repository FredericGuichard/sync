### Experimental design

#### Treatments

```
- Meadow (center, outside)
- Block (nested within Meadow)
- Transplant (resident, transplanted)
- Defaunation (live, defaunated)
- Control
```

#### Sample size

- 2 Meadow \* 5  Blocks \* (2 Transplants \* 2 Defaunations + 1 control) = 50 cores
- You could have a minimum of 4 Blocks, but I would prioritize getting 5 Blocks

![[samplingDesign.png]]

#### Semi-random site selection criteria

- Blocks and cores within blocks need to be randomly selected
- selection criteria:
  - Need to list key environmental conditions and their acceptable range to accept a valid Block or core within each Block
- Selection process. Either:
  - Identify more Blocks and core positions than needed, and select randomly among these
  - Randomly select Blocks and cores, and only accept those that match criteria.

### Methodological aspects

#### Defaunation

- Ex-situ freezing works but crystal formation can affect sediment and biogeochemistry of sediments. It will also leave a few resistant species alive.
