### Transient pulse-press dynamics

\[@jaggiTransientDynamicsNonlinear2026] develop a  Transient Response Matrix (TRM) from matrix population models that can decompose the transient effects of repeated pulse disturbance (of demographic rates, not of abundances) combined with press disturbances. Applied to structured population models but could potentially be adapted to community dynamics (Markov chain succession models). [[jaggiTransientDynamicsNonlinear2026]]
