# Feasibility

###### seagrass restoration is based on transplants

###### lethal heat stress has been used (microwave)

# Challenges

###### enclosed mesocosms with limited natural colonization and fluxes

###### Manipulation without loosing too much material

# Hypotheses

- Sediment type affects community structure and ecosystem functions
- environmental conditions interact with sediment type to affect the relationship between community structure and ecosystem functions [rodilPositiveContributionMacrofaunal](zotero://select/library/items/9VGL6YYS)
- Sediment type has an effect on functional groups abundance in endobenthic community
- Organic matter content affects trophic position in the endobenthic community
- the relative abundance of [[Core transplant experiment#^3df130|macro-detritivores]] affects decomposition rates [[sagiRevisitingDecompositionTheory2026]]

# Treatments

###### Reciprocal transplants from low and high environmental stress areas

###### Sediment manipulation by

- Massive mortality (freezing)
- Fine sediment removal with or without mortality
- Addition of organic and/or non-organic material
- Neighbourhood effects (Shuaishuai)

###### Inoculation

- Adding functional groups under meshed lid (mussels, polychaetes, natural and artificial eelgrass leaves)
- litter bags [[sagiRevisitingDecompositionTheory2026]] with mesh size controlling for acces to macro-detritivores^3df130
