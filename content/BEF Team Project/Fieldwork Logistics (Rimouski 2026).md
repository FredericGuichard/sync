## Pre-departure reminders

### equipment checklist

#### UQAR guidelines

- Make sure we replace any damaged equipment borrowed from UQAR
- Make sure UQAR members won't need any equipment you are borrowing
- In doubt purchase additional small items (e.g. sieves, cores) that could be useful for UQAR collaborators
- Rinse all equipment used in salt water after each use (no matter the material or value).

#### Travel preparation

- Travel registry
- Car, opt for full insurance coverage and use McGill [Travel Services](https://www.mcgill.ca/travelservices/transport/book-vehicleTravel)
- Field work resources:
  - CEEB (BIology) checklist ![[CEEBFieldworkChecklistDraft31032025.docx]]

#### Finances

- Covered expenses
  - transport (car rental, gas)
  - lodging
  - meals during travel to/from Montreal
  - Field and lab supplies and equipment
  - Field outdoor gear (waders, gloves, etc.)
- I recommend asking for a travel advance
- Consult with me before purchase of expensive items (>\$1000)
- report on total expenses after each trip
- File expense report after each trip if you havent's requested a travel advance

#### Safety

- You wil need [SIMDUT online training](https://www.mcgill.ca/ehs/safety-training/whmis) if you haven't completed it
- Never go to field sites alone, 2 people minimum
- Field sites are not remote, but carry you phone and emergency numbers
- Manage your energy. Tides and fieldwork in general impose irregular hours but make sure to take breaks.
